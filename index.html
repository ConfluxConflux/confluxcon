Agenda

I'm renting out Building B of Lighthaven for the whole day! I expect things to be low-key in the morning/afternoon, with most of the programming and socializing in the evening.

There will be a variety of snacks, and probably some form of dinner. There might be lunch, depending on interest.

Opt-in activities

Here are some activities that might happen, depending on interest.

    You are free (and encouraged!) to run your own activities. I've always found running my own 1-hour-ish activities to be a delightful aspect of Lighthaven unconferences (it's such an easy place to run things because you'll have a built-in room and built-in audience) so if you have any ideas, this is a great time!

    Theory of impact

    by having fun and making memories, we will increase our positive associations with living in berkeley. this will increase the chance that we live in berkeley in the future, which will increase the chance that we do impactful ai safety work, which will decrease the chance ai kills everyone, etc…

    also, it seems good to have fun in life regardless. and you get to say goodbye to me before i move back to the far-off land of menlo park, california!
